1. Open **`.env`** file in Notepad
2. Replace `your_anthropic_api_key_here` with your real key
   - Get it here: https://console.anthropic.com/settings/keys

3. Save and close

### Step 3: Start Your Bot
Double-click: **`START_BOT.bat`**

✅ Done. Your bot is live.

## How It Works

- Bot runs **only when your PC is on**
- All data saved to `G:\Claude\Manifestation Bot\data\`
- When PC is off, Telegram messages wait for you
- When PC is back on, double-click `START_BOT.bat` again
- All your entries are still there - nothing is lost

## Auto-Start with Windows (Optional)

Want the bot to start automatically when you turn on your PC?

1. Press `Win + R`
2. Type `shell:startup` and press Enter
3. Copy `START_BOT.bat` into that folder
4. Done

Now the bot starts every time Windows starts.
